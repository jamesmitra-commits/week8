How to Spot an IoT Device

🕵️ Year 3 Detective Checklist: Ask 3 Questions!

1️⃣

Does it have Sensors?

Can it see light, feel temperature, or scan barcodes?

2️⃣

Is it Connected?

Does it connect to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular internet?

3️⃣

Does it Share Data?

Does it send info automatically to help people?

If the answer is YES to all 3 — You found an IoT Device! 🎉
